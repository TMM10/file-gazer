A note about sizes.

The map measures logical file sizes: the length of files reported by Windows, added into their parent folders. Twice the bytes means twice the bubble area. Compare siblings within the same folder or group; deeper bubbles use local scales while their parent remains as context.

Scroll to zoom smoothly around your pointer, drag to move, or click a bubble to bring it into focus. Smaller folders and files emerge as you move closer.

File sizes can differ from occupied disk space because of compression, sparse files, hard links, protected folders and file-system metadata. Drive bars use Windows' capacity figures.

Scans skip inaccessible folders and links or junctions. A skipped path does not mean an empty folder. Categories are inferred from location and extension; game names come from launcher manifests or recognizable installation folders.
